Online Food Ordering
Data Visualization

Exploratory analysis of customer demographics, ordering behavior, and feedback patterns.
Source: Kaggle — Online Food Ordering Dataset

Chart 1: Distribution of Customer Age

This chart shows the age range of customers who placed online food orders. Most customers fall between 22 and 25 years old, indicating a younger customer base.

Chart 2: Orders by Occupation (Reorder Status)

This chart breaks down orders by customer occupation and whether they reordered. Students place the most orders, with a high reorder rate, followed by employees and self-employed customers.

Chart 3: Monthly Income vs Reorder Status

This chart compares reorder rates across income brackets. Customers with no income (largely students) account for the most orders, while reorder rates stay high across all income levels.

Chart 4: Feedback Distribution by Gender

This chart shows positive and negative feedback split by gender. Both male and female customers left mostly positive feedback, with males providing slightly more positive responses overall.
