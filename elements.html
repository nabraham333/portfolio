Assignment 3: World Happiness Report — Viz Critique

Data Source: World Population Review — Happiest Countries in the World

Good Data Visualization

Why it works: The horizontal bar chart cleanly presents the top 10 happiest nations ordered by score. It features a high data-to-ink ratio, zero visual clutter, readable labels, and a consistent color scheme that enables quick visual comparisons.

Bad Data Visualization

Why it fails: The 3D pie chart uses too many categories (slices), distorted proportions due to 3D perspective, non-intuitive color choices, and unreadable text labels that mislead the audience and increase cognitive load.
